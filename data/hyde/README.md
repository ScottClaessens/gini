# Information on dataset `hyde.csv`

**File name:** `hyde.csv`

**Title:** Time-series estimates of land use and population sizes from the HYDE v3.5 dataset for 972 longitude-latitude coordinates

**Authors:** Kees Klein Goldewijk

**Reference:** Klein Goldewijk, K., Beusen, A., Doelman, J., & van der Linden, M. (2026). Holocene anthropogenic land-use reconstruction HYDE 3.5, Data in Brief, submitted.

**Source data URL:** <https://public.yoda.uu.nl/geo/UU01/F45D44.html>

**HYDE project URL:** <https://landuse.sites.uu.nl/hyde-project/>

**Date accessed:** 22nd June 2026

**Dimensions:** 54,432 rows x 19 columns

**Notes:** The `hyde.csv` dataset is generated by the R script `extract_hyde_data.R`. This script loops over time slices in the HYDE dataset (-10,000 BC to 1980) and extracts time-series estimates of land use and population sizes based on longitude-latitude coordinates for archaeological sites in the Gini dataset. The resulting dataset is in long format with 54,432 rows. See `extract_hyde_data.R` for more details.

**Data dictionary:**

- `date` - dated year of the estimate (negative = BC, positive = AD), for linking to the Gini dataset
- `longitude` - longitude in decimal degrees, for linking to the Gini dataset
- `latitude` - latitude in decimal degrees, for linking to the Gini dataset
- `popc` - total population count (number of inhabitants)
- `rurc` - total rural population count (number of inhabitants)
- `urbc` - total urban population count (number of inhabitants)
- `uopp` - total built-up area, such as towns, cities, etc, in km2
- `cropland` - total cropland area in km2
- `grazing` - total land used for grazing in km2
- `pasture` - total pasture area in km2
- `rangeland` - total rangeland area in km2
- `conv_rangeland` - total converted rangeland area in km2
- `ir_norice` - irrigated other crops (no rice) area in km2
- `rf_norice` - rainfed other crops (no rice) area in km2
- `ir_rice` - irrigated rice area in km2
- `rf_rice` - rainfed rice area in km2
- `tot_irri` - total actual irrigated area in km2
- `tot_rainfed` - total rainfed area in km2
- `tot_rice` - total rice area in km2
